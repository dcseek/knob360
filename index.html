How it works

Three moves, zero menu diving

No touchscreen maze. The knob is the interface.

Turn to navigate

Rotate the knob to move between pages — CLIMATE DOWN, CLIMATE UP, THERMOSTAT, LIGHTS. The ring changes color with every page.

Click to lock

Press the knob to fix the page. The "ADJUST" label appears: now the rotation controls the value, not the page.

Turn to adjust

Rotate to change temperature in 0.5 °C steps. Home Assistant confirms with a green flash and OK! — or red if something fails.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to write code?

No. You install the ESPHome add-on, copy the files, adapt the entity names to yours (the guide marks every spot with grep-friendly comments), compile and flash. About 10 minutes the first time. A white-glove service (we compile the firmware for you with your entities) is available on request.

What does it control?

Three climate zones (two air conditioners + one thermostat) and up to nine lights with individual toggles and all-on/all-off. Pages, entities and colors are fully customizable in the config.

Is the door lock included?

No — deliberately. The commercial firmware intentionally excludes lock control. Safety first.

Which hardware is required?

The Elecrow CrowPanel 1.46″ HMI (model DHR55146D, ESP32-S3 with 8 MB PSRAM). The pack is software only — the hardware is not included.

What if something doesn't work?

Message me on eBay. Setup support is included in the price, and the guide covers the common failure modes (display init, service calls, Wi-Fi).
